# Harborline Refund and Returns Policy

Effective July 1, 2026. This policy applies to orders placed on the Harborline web store.

## 1. Returning an item you changed your mind about
- You can return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a refund of the item price.
- **Electronics** that have been opened can be returned within **15 days of delivery**. Unopened electronics follow the 30-day rule.
- Items marked **Final Sale** can't be returned for a change of mind.
- The original shipping fee is not refunded when you change your mind.

## 2. Damaged, defective or wrong items
- If an item arrives damaged, stops working because of a defect, or isn't what you ordered, tell us within **30 days of delivery**.
- We refund the item price **and** the order's shipping fee. This applies to Final Sale items too.

## 3. Cancelling an order
- An order that has not shipped yet can be cancelled for a **full refund**, shipping included.
- Once an order has shipped, it can't be cancelled. Return it instead when it arrives.

## 4. Shipping fees
- Orders of **$75 or more** (before shipping) ship free. If we charged shipping on such an order, we refund the fee.
- If a delivery arrives **more than 5 days after** the promised date, we refund the shipping fee.

## 5. How refunds are paid
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days.
- Orders paid with a Harborline gift card are refunded as store credit.

## 6. Approval rules for support staff
- A refund **above $500** must be approved by a support lead. Support agents hand these cases to the support-leads queue.
- Accounts on **review hold** can't receive refunds until a support lead clears the hold.

## 7. Talking to a person
- Customers can ask to speak to a person at any time.
