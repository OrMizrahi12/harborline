# Pinecrest Home adds a $5.95 return fee, waives it for members

Home Retail Daily · Jon Achebe · 2026-08-27

Pinecrest Home, an online home goods retailer, will charge a $5.95 fee for return shipping from September 15.

The fee will be waived for members of its loyalty program, for damaged or defective items, and for returns dropped off at partner locations.

A Pinecrest spokesperson said the change "keeps returns free for our most loyal customers while reflecting the real cost of shipping."

Pinecrest did not say how many of its customers are loyalty members.
