# Warehouse operations note: returns processing

Harborline Fulfillment · Fulfillment operations · 2026-06-30

Average time from a return arriving at the warehouse to the refund being issued: 6.5 days (target: 4 days).

22% of returned items could not be resold as new. Most were opened electronics and bedding.

Returns peak in January and in the two weeks after any sale event. During peaks, the returns team needs four extra temporary staff.

A drop-off network would let us receive returns in consolidated shipments, which would make staffing easier to plan.
