# Return fraud and abuse: annual survey of loss-prevention managers

Northgate Loss Prevention Council · Northgate staff · 2025-12-10

Loss-prevention managers estimate that 9.4% of online returns in 2025 involved fraud or policy abuse, up from 8.1% in 2024.

The most common forms reported:
- Wardrobing: using an item once and returning it (named by 64% of managers).
- Returning a different, cheaper item than the one bought (31%).
- Claiming a parcel never arrived (29%).

Managers at retailers with free returns were more likely to report rising abuse (57%) than those at retailers that charge a fee (39%).

Method: survey of 180 loss-prevention managers at North American retailers, October 2025. Estimates are the managers' own and vary widely.
