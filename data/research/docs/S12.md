# Return policy leniency and customer lifetime value

Lakeshore University School of Business (working paper) · M. Okafor and L. Brandt · 2025-04-15

We study how lenient return policies relate to customer lifetime value (CLV) using data from 23 online retailers between 2019 and 2024.

In home goods, customers acquired under a free-returns policy had an 8% higher three-year CLV than customers acquired under a paid-returns policy, after controlling for first-order size and channel.

The effect was smaller for low-priced items (under $40) and not significant for electronics.

Limitations: retailers chose their own policies, so the comparison is not a randomized experiment. This is a working paper and has not been peer reviewed.
