# The bracketing habit: shoppers buy several sizes on purpose

Consumer Pulse Panel · Elena Sorvino · 2025-09-02

41% of online shoppers say they bought several sizes or colors of the same item in the past year, planning to send back the ones they didn't keep. Shoppers call it bracketing.

Bracketing is most common in clothing (34% of shoppers did it) but appears in home goods too: 12% bought two versions of a rug, lamp or bedding set to compare them at home.

Free returns make bracketing possible. Asked what they would do if a store added a $5 return fee:
- 58% said they would be very or somewhat likely to abandon the cart.
- 23% said they would buy only one version.
- 19% said it would make no difference.

Method: online survey, July 2025, 2,050 adults in the United States who bought something online in the past three months. Margin of error about 2 points.
