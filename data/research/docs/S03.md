# E-commerce Returns Benchmark 2023

Meridian Commerce Institute · Meridian research team · 2024-01-22

Key finding: the average online return rate in 2023 was 16.5% of units sold.

The 2023 benchmark covers 986 merchants and about 37 million orders for the full calendar year.

Return rate by category (share of units returned):
- Apparel and footwear: 25.1%
- Home goods: 11.2%
- Consumer electronics: 9.5%

The share of merchants offering free return shipping on every order rose from 49% in 2021 to 62% in 2023.

As in earlier years, we count units, not order value.
