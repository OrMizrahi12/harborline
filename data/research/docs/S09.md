# Customer Survey: Delivery and Returns (August 2026)

Harborline Customer Insights · Customer Insights team · 2026-09-05

We asked 1,148 customers who ordered in the past six months about delivery and returns.

- 61% said free returns influenced their decision to buy their last order from Harborline.
- If we added a $4.95 return shipping fee, 27% said they would shop with us less often. 9% said they would stop shopping with us.
- Among Plus members, 35% said they would shop less often, compared with 24% of other customers.
- 72% said a fee would be fair for "changed my mind" returns if damaged or wrong items stayed free.

Customers who returned something in the past six months were more sensitive to a fee than customers who had not.
