# Q2 2026 Returns Report

Harborline Operations · Harborline Operations team · 2026-07-20

Summary for April to June 2026.

Return rate
- By order value: 19.2%
- By units: 13.1%
The value rate is higher because furniture and electronics, our most expensive items, are returned more often than kitchen and bedding items.

Cost per return: $11.40 on average
- Return shipping: $7.10
- Handling and inspection: $2.60
- Items written off (not resellable): $1.70

Top return reasons (share of returns)
- Changed my mind: 38%
- Arrived damaged: 17%
- Didn't match the description: 15%
- Wrong size or fit: 14%
- Other: 16%

Return volume grew 9% compared with Q2 2025, faster than orders (6%).
