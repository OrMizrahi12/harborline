# E-commerce Returns Benchmark 2025

Meridian Commerce Institute · Meridian research team · 2025-11-04

Key finding: the average online return rate in the first nine months of 2025 was 17.6% of units sold.

This benchmark is built from transaction records, not from surveys. 1,214 merchants shared anonymized order and return data covering January to September 2025, about 48 million orders in total.

Return rate by category (share of units returned):
- Apparel and footwear: 26.4%
- Home goods: 11.9%
- Consumer electronics: 9.8%
- Beauty: 5.1%

Merchants that offered free return shipping on every order had a return rate 3.4 points higher than merchants that charged a fee, after controlling for category.

Why our number may differ from survey figures: we count units, not value. Expensive items are returned more often than cheap ones, so return rates measured by order value are usually several points higher than rates measured by units.
