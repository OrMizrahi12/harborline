# What happened when retailers started charging for returns

Retail Signals Weekly · Marcus Feld · 2026-05-19

Last year, 40 retailers in our panel added a return shipping fee of between $3.95 and $5.95. We compared them with 40 similar retailers that kept returns free.

Six months after the change:
- Return volume fell by a median of 18% at the retailers that added a fee.
- Their repeat purchase rate (customers who bought again within six months) fell by 6%, against 1% for the comparison group.
- Customer service contacts about returns rose by about a quarter, mostly complaints about the fee.

Most retailers that charged a fee waived it in some cases: for damaged or wrong items (38 of 40) and for loyalty program members (22 of 40).

"The fee paid for itself in shipping savings," said the finance director of a home goods retailer, "but we are still measuring what it did to loyalty."

Method: Retailers self-selected into the panel. The comparison group was matched by category and size. Results are medians.
