# Finance memo: the cost of free returns, FY2026 forecast

Harborline Finance · Harborline Finance · 2026-08-14

Return shipping is forecast to cost Harborline $1.94 million in fiscal year 2026, up from $1.61 million in FY2025.

We modeled a $4.95 return fee on "changed my mind" returns, waived for damaged, defective or wrong items and for Plus members:
- If return volume falls 15%, the fee and the lower volume together save between $0.6 million and $0.8 million a year.
- The model does not include any loss of repeat purchases. A 2% drop in repeat purchases would cost about $0.5 million in gross margin.

Recommendation: test the fee on one category before rolling it out.
