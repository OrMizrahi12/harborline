# Drop-off points cut the cost of return shipping

Parcel & Post Review · Hannah Iqbal · 2026-07-08

Box-free drop-off points, where shoppers hand over an unpacked item and a QR code, are changing the cost of returns.

Retailers using drop-off networks paid an average of $5.70 to bring back one parcel in the first half of 2026, compared with $8.20 for a prepaid label and a box. The networks consolidate many returns into one shipment back to the warehouse.

Returned items also reach the warehouse about two days sooner, which matters for seasonal goods that lose value quickly.

The catch: coverage. Drop-off points are dense in cities but thin in rural areas, so retailers that switch still need a mail-back option.
