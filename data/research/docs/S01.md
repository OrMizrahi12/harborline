# Online return rates hit a new high as free returns become the norm

Retail Signals Weekly · Dana Whitfield · 2026-03-12

Online shoppers sent back more of what they bought in 2025 than in any year we have tracked. In our annual survey of 312 retail executives, the average online return rate reached 24.0% of order value, up from 21.3% the year before.

The rise is not spread evenly. Apparel retailers reported rates above 30%, while home goods retailers reported 15.2%. Electronics sat close to the average.

Executives named the same driver again and again: free returns. 71% of the retailers in the survey now offer free return shipping on every order, and most of them said they could not stop without losing customers to a competitor.

"Free returns started as a promotion and turned into an expectation," one chief operating officer told us. "Now the question is who pays for it."

Return costs are rising too. Respondents estimated that processing one online return costs between $9 and $14 once shipping, handling and items that can't be resold are counted.

Method: The survey ran from November 2025 to January 2026. Return rate is the share of online order value that was returned, as reported by each retailer. Figures are self-reported and were not audited.
