# Half of everything bought online gets sent back

The Checkout Line (personal blog) · anonymous · 2026-02-03

Here is a number nobody in retail wants to say out loud: about half of everything people buy online gets sent back.

I worked in a returns warehouse for two holiday seasons, and some weeks it felt like more came in than went out. Free returns are a disaster for the planet and for small shops.

If you run a store, charge for returns. Your customers will get over it.
